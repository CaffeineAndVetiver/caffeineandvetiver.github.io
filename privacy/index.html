Caffeine & Vetiver - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

This policy covers the Pinterest integration used by Caffeine & Vetiver, the Etsy shop at etsy.com/shop/CaffeineAndVetiver.

What this tool is

This is a single-user internal tool. It does not collect, store, or process any personal data from Pinterest users, site visitors, or customers. It uses the Pinterest API solely to create pins on our own Pinterest business account, linking to our own publicly listed Etsy products. No third-party data is accessed, collected, or shared.

Use of the Pinterest API

Caffeine & Vetiver uses the Pinterest API. Caffeine & Vetiver is an independent business and is not endorsed by, sponsored by, affiliated with, or otherwise associated with Pinterest, Inc.

Pinterest data and disconnecting

The only Pinterest data this tool holds is an OAuth access token for our own Pinterest account, together with the board and pin identifiers that Pinterest returns when a pin is created. We do not store Pinterest user profiles, follower lists, analytics, or any other Pinterest content.

If the account is disconnected, or access is revoked from Pinterest account settings, we delete the stored access token and every piece of Pinterest-derived data associated with it, including the stored board and pin identifiers. Deletion happens at the point of disconnection, and no backup copies are kept.

No resale or redistribution

We do not sell, resell, license, redistribute, or otherwise share Pinterest content or Pinterest-derived data with any third party.
